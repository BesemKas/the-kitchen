# Open Food Facts data

`off-za.jsonl.gz` holds the products Open Food Facts lists as sold in South Africa, one per line, in the shape of their product API. It's built by `.github/workflows/off-za.yml` from their Parquet export (monthly, or by hand), and loaded into the app's shared catalogue under *Me → Running the app → Load South African products*.

Source: Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org), made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/. Individual contents are under the Database Contents License. This file is a derived database and is shared under the same licence.
